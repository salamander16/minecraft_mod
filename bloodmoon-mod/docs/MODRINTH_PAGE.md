# 🌑 Blood Moon

**Every 20th night, the moon turns red - and the night fights back.**

A fully **server-side** Fabric mod. Players join with a **vanilla client**.

## What changes on a Blood Moon night

- 🔴 **Red sky, fog, clouds and night lighting** (no resource pack needed)
- 👥 **Doubled monster spawns**
- 💪 **Tougher mobs** with long-range tracking
- 💎 **Overpowered loot** - bonus XP, diamonds, golden apples, netherite scrap, and rare totems
- 💥 **Creepers are fast**
- 🕷️ **Spiders are invisible**
- 🏹 **Some skeletons (all kinds) carry explosive bows**
- 🧟 **Zombies wear enchanted diamond/netherite armour and smash through doors, fence gates, fences and trapdoors**
- 🔱 **Drowned carry Channeling tridents** - during a thunderstorm

## Setup

Install **Fabric API**, drop the jar into `mods/`, done. Everything is configurable in `config/bloodmoon.json`.

## Commands (ops)

`/bloodmoon status` · `/bloodmoon start` · `/bloodmoon stop` · `/bloodmoon reload`

## Notes

Server-side mods cannot recolour the moon sprite itself - the sky, fog and clouds turn red instead.
